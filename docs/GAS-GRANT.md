## A note on key hygiene

The four demo keys were generated by `scripts/keygen.ts` and live in `.env`.
They are throwaway keys holding faucet funds, which is fine for testnet, but the
deployer will now hold real BOT.

The demo signing route cannot spend it: `/api/act` refuses any chain other than
968 in code, and only calls whitelisted functions on our own contracts. The
residual exposure is ordinary environment-variable risk on the host.

If you would rather not have the mainnet-funded key in a hosting environment at
all, generate a separate deployer for mainnet and give that address on the form
instead, then set only `APPLICANT_`, `AGENT_` and `VALIDATOR_` keys on Vercel
and let the demo buttons that need the principal fall back to wallet signing.
For roughly nine dollars of gas, either choice is defensible.
